Experiment: 02_augmentation

Goal:

Evaluate impact of data augmentation on generalization

Expected:

- higher validation F1
- reduced overfitting
- more stable training curves

Change:

- Added data augmentation:
  - Horizontal flip
  - Vertical flip
  - Random rotation (±30°)
  - Color jitter (brightness, contrast, saturation)

Hypothesis:

- Improved generalization
- Reduced overfitting
- Higher validation F1 compared to baseline (~0.46)

Training Strategy:

- Training was stopped early after observing no improvement beyond epoch 4–10
- Model reached peak performance quickly due to augmentation
- Further training led to instability and overfitting

Insight:

- Augmentation improves data efficiency (faster convergence)
- Optimal training duration is significantly reduced compared to baseline
