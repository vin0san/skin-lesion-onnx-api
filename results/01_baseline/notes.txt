Experiment Name: 01_baseline

Goal:
Establish baseline performance with pretrained EfficientNet-B0 without augmentation

Expectation:
Model will overfit due to small dataset and lack of augmentation.
Minority classes will have lower recall due to imbalance.

Model Selection Metric:
Macro F1-score

WHY:
Ensures balanced performance across all classes and prevents bias toward majority classes in an imbalanced medical dataset.

Training Setup:

- Loss: CrossEntropyLoss
- Optimizer: AdamW (lr=1e-4)
- Metric: Macro F1 (model selection)
- Additional metric: Balanced Accuracy

Final Observation (Baseline):

- Training loss decreased consistently (2.28 → ~0.12)
- Validation loss increased steadily after early epochs, indicating overfitting
- Macro F1 improved and peaked at epoch 17 (0.4595)

Insights:

- Overfitting began early (around epoch 4–5), but did not immediately degrade performance
- Model continued to extract useful features even after initial overfitting phase
- Performance remained unstable due to small dataset size and class imbalance
- Validation loss was not aligned with model performance (F1 continued improving)

Visualization Insights:

- Training loss decreased steadily
- Validation loss increased after early epochs → overfitting
- F1 peaked at epoch 17 and fluctuated afterward
- Training was unstable due to small dataset and imbalance

Conclusion:

- Best model occurs at epoch 17 (not final epoch)
- Early stopping based on validation loss alone would have missed optimal performance
- Strong need for regularization (augmentation) to stabilize training and improve generalization
