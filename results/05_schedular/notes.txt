Experiment: 05_scheduler_resolution

Changes:

- Increased input resolution (224 → 300)
- Added ReduceLROnPlateau scheduler
- Kept augmentation and class weighting

Hypothesis:

- Higher resolution improves feature extraction (lesion details)
- Scheduler improves convergence and stability
- Potential improvement in Macro F1 beyond previous best (~0.53)

Final Observation (Scheduler + Resolution):

- Training loss decreased steadily with smoother convergence
- Validation loss remained lower compared to previous experiments (~1.5–1.9)
- Macro F1 improved significantly and peaked at 0.5857 (epoch 12)

Insights:

- ReduceLROnPlateau scheduler enabled finer optimization by reducing learning rate after plateau
- Higher input resolution improved feature extraction, especially lesion boundary details
- Training became more stable with fewer fluctuations
- Model benefited from longer training compared to previous experiments

Comparison with Previous Experiments:

- Baseline F1 ≈ 0.46
- Augmentation F1 ≈ 0.51
- Aug + Weights F1 ≈ 0.53
- Final Model F1 ≈ 0.586 (best)

Conclusion:

- Combining augmentation, class weighting, scheduler, and higher resolution yields best performance
- Scheduler is critical for extracting final performance gains
- Higher resolution is beneficial for medical imaging tasks
